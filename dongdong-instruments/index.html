DongDong Instruments — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 Developer: mystic13 Contact: chen_sxue@163.com

DongDong Instruments (咚咚乐器) is an offline instrument discovery app for children and families. It includes pictures, spoken instrument names and short videos. No account or registration is required.

Data stored on the device

The app saves your language choice and the IDs of instruments you have discovered in the app’s local storage. These records let you keep your preferred language and see discovered instruments in color. They are not sent to us or to a server. The Android app disables automatic app backup.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, device identifiers, analytics, usage histories, location, photos, contacts or recordings. There are no advertising, analytics, account, payment or social media services in the app. There are no in-app purchases.

Offline media and permissions

Pictures, name recordings and videos are bundled with the installation. Normal use does not require an internet connection or a remote speech service. The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or photo-library access. Name recordings are prepared before distribution; the app does not record your voice.

Children and families

Children can explore the included media without creating an account or sending personal information. The parent area contains language choices, media information and this policy. Parents and caregivers may contact us using the email above; sending an email is optional and occurs outside the app. Any information you choose to include in an email is used to respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

Language and discovery records remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage resets the saved language and discoveries. We do not hold a server copy of these records.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. This policy describes the app; operating systems and app stores have their own privacy practices.

咚咚乐器 — 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日 开发者：mystic13 联系邮箱：chen_sxue@163.com

咚咚乐器是一款供孩子与家人共同探索乐器的离线应用，内含图片、乐器名称语音和短视频，无需账号或注册。

本机保存的数据

应用仅在本机保存语言选择和已发现乐器的标识，用于恢复所选语言和在图鉴中显示已发现乐器的彩色图片。这些记录不会发送给开发者或服务器。Android 版已关闭应用自动备份。

收集与共享

应用不收集、传输、出售或共享个人信息、设备标识符、分析数据、使用历史、位置、照片、通讯录或录音。应用无广告、分析、账号、支付或社交服务，也无应用内购买。

离线媒体与权限

图片、名称语音和视频随安装包提供，日常使用无需联网或调用远程语音服务。应用不申请麦克风、摄像头、位置、通讯录或相册访问权限。名称语音在发布前制作，应用不会录制用户的声音。

儿童与家庭

孩子无需创建账号或发送个人信息即可探索内置媒体。家长区提供语言选择、媒体来源和本政策。家长可自愿通过上述邮箱联系开发者；邮件在应用外发送，主动提供的邮件内容仅用于答复相应请求。

保留与删除

语言与发现记录保存在本机，清除应用存储空间或卸载应用后会删除。清除存储会重置语言和发现记录。开发者不保存这些记录的服务器副本。

变更

如数据处理方式改变，本政策将更新生效日期。本政策说明本应用的数据处理方式，操作系统和应用商店适用各自的隐私政策。
