本政策说明记忆探险队 Android 应用如何使用和保存信息。应用面向小学阶段的儿童；请监护人与孩子一起阅读。

应用在设备上使用的信息

应用会读取设备的语言设置，用它选择初始界面语言。用户也可以手动选择语言，并开启或关闭音效；这两项选择保存在当前设备上。音效由应用内置文件播放，不需要联网。

为了显示已完成关卡、卡片收藏、继续故事和安排回想，应用在当前设备上保存关卡进度与成绩、尝试次数、连续完成情况、完成及下次回想日期。故事玩法还保存所用的图卡或题目编号、首次回想时选择的答案、卡片之间选择的连接动作、提示次数、完成时间和用时。其他五种玩法保存最近一次的用时和成绩，用于与同一关的下一次游玩比较。卡片收藏状态由本机通关与星级记录得出，不另建账户或上传收藏记录。首次操作提示是否已看过，也保存在设备上。旧玩法另有可选的「我的画面」短句输入，用户手动输入的部分最多 80 个字符；短句及选择的提示词会随该关记录保存在设备上。请勿在短句中填写姓名、电话、地址或其他个人信息。

“记忆游戏岛”会在本机保存每关游玩次数、最近和最好星级、完成日期、最近一次用时，以及获得三星时的最好用时。星星总数、世界徽章和满星游戏收藏卡都由这些本机记录计算；每个游戏的 8 关最好星级均为三星时，卡片册显示该游戏的卡片，不另外保存卡片状态。游戏没有倒计时；不上传这些记录。

这些信息只用于运行游戏、展示个人进度、恢复本机记录和安排本机回想。应用不要求创建账户。

传输、共享与第三方服务

本发布版本不把上述记录发送给运营者或第三方服务器，也不把它们出售或分享给其他应用。应用未接入广告、使用行为统计或远程崩溃上报服务。故事动画由应用内置文件在设备上的 WebView 中播放，不请求远程故事内容。

保存、安全与删除

记录保存在 Android 为本应用分配的私有存储空间中，受设备系统的应用隔离机制保护。此发布版本关闭 Android 应用数据备份，并在数据提取规则中排除云备份和 Android 设备间迁移。应用没有 iOS 对应版本，也未配置跨平台迁移。因此更换设备或重装应用时，进度不会自动恢复。此处不表示设备本身的屏幕锁或操作系统能提供绝对安全。

本机记录会保留到用户清除应用数据或卸载应用为止；普通应用更新通常会保留它们。要删除记录，请到 Android 「设置 > 应用 > 记忆探险队 > 存储」中选择「清除数据／清除存储空间」，也可以卸载应用。这会删除该设备上的语言与音效选择、进度、成绩及短句，且应用无法恢复。应用没有服务器端账户或记录，运营者无法远程查看、恢复或删除当前设备上的数据。若曾使用允许系统备份的旧版本，旧备份由相应设备或备份服务管理，需通过该服务的设置处理。

儿童与政策变更

应用不设公开聊天、社交分享或定向广告，也不要求孩子提交姓名、邮箱等身份资料。若今后增加账户、联网、广告、统计、备份或其他改变信息处理方式的功能，我们会先更新本政策和相应的 Google Play 数据披露。

This policy explains how the Memory Explorers Android app uses and stores information. The app is designed for primary-school-age children. We encourage parents and guardians to read this policy with children.

Information used on the device

The app reads the device language setting to choose an initial interface language. Users may choose another language and turn sound effects on or off. Both choices are saved on the current device. Sound effects play from files bundled with the app and need no internet connection.

To show completed levels, collected cards, ongoing stories, and scheduled recall activities, the app saves level progress and scores, attempt counts, consecutive successes, completion dates, and next recall dates on the current device. For story activities, it also saves card or task identifiers, the answers chosen on the first recall attempt, actions chosen to connect cards, the number of hints used, completion time, and duration. The five other play modes save the latest duration and score to compare with the next attempt at the same level. Collected cards are derived from local completion and star records; they do not require an account or a separate uploaded record. The app saves whether first-use hints have been shown. In an older set of activities, users may optionally enter a short “my picture” note. The part typed by the user is limited to 80 characters; the note and any selected prompt word are saved with that level on the device. Please do not enter a name, phone number, address, or other personal information in the note.

Memory Game Island saves each level’s play count, latest and best star rating, completion date, latest duration, and best duration for a three-star result on this device. Star totals, world badges, and collectible cards for fully starred games are derived from these local records. A card appears in the album when all eight levels of that game have a best rating of three stars; no separate card status is saved. The games have no countdown and do not upload these records.

The app uses this information only to run the activities, show personal progress, restore records on the same device, and schedule on-device recall. No account is required.

Transmission, sharing, and third-party services

This release does not send those records to the operator or a third-party server, sell them, or share them with other apps. It does not include advertising, usage analytics, or remote crash-reporting services. Story animations run from files bundled with the app in an on-device WebView and do not request remote story content.

Storage, security, and deletion

Records are stored in the private storage area assigned to this app by Android and protected by the operating system's app isolation. This release disables Android app-data backup and excludes cloud backup and Android device-to-device transfer through its data extraction rules. The app has no iOS counterpart and does not configure cross-platform transfer. Progress therefore will not automatically return after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. This does not mean that the device screen lock or operating system provides absolute security.

Local records remain until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls it; ordinary app updates generally preserve them. To delete the records, use Android Settings > Apps > Memory Explorers > Storage > Clear storage/Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes the language and sound choices, progress, scores, and notes from that device, and the app cannot restore them. The app has no server account or server copy of these records. The operator cannot remotely view, restore, or delete data held on the user's device. If an earlier app version allowed a system backup, any older backup is managed by the relevant device or backup service and should be handled through that service's settings.

Children and policy changes

The app does not provide public chat, social sharing, or targeted ads, and it does not ask children for a name, email address, or other identity details. If accounts, network features, ads, analytics, backup, or other changes to information handling are added later, we will update this policy and the corresponding Google Play disclosures first.

For privacy questions, contact: chen_sxue@163.com.
