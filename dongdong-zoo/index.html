应用内的数据处理

咚咚动物园无需注册或登录。24 种动物的插画、预录名称语音和叫声随应用安装。触摸、切换动物、静音和轮播在设备本地完成；家长选择的界面语言由应用保存在设备设置中，用于下次打开时恢复。当前版本不通过应用向开发者或第三方传送姓名、电子邮箱、位置、设备标识符、操作记录、照片或录音，也不出售或共享这些数据。

应用没有广告、分析或跟踪工具，也没有应用内购买。名称语音和叫声均为预置录音；应用不调用麦克风，也不请求相机或定位权限。应用内容可离线使用。

儿童与家长

本应用面向 2 岁以上孩子与陪同的家长。应用不要求儿童提供个人资料，没有聊天、上传或社交分享功能。家长可协助孩子操作并控制设备音量。

保存、删除与安全

应用不向开发者服务器发送或保存用户资料，因此开发者没有应用账户或互动记录可供检索。界面语言选择可在应用内改为跟随系统语言，也可通过清除应用数据或卸载应用删除本地副本。若设备启用了 Android 系统备份，系统可能按用户的备份设置将该偏好备份到云端或迁移到新设备；系统备份副本由用户通过设备或账户的备份设置管理。应用自身没有个人数据上传至开发者服务器的环节。

如您主动发邮件至 chen_sxue@163.com 咨询，您的邮箱地址及邮件内容会经邮件服务处理。我们仅用它回复和处理请求，不将其用于广告。仅在处理咨询所需期间保留这些邮件；请求完成后删除，适用法律另有要求的除外。您可通过同一地址请求查阅或删除该往来邮件。我们限制邮箱访问，并使用邮件服务商提供的安全措施。

外部服务和更新

Google Play 的下载、安装和商店服务按 Google 自身政策处理。若您主动打开本网页，网页由 GitHub Pages 提供，访问该网页可能涉及 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理技术访问数据；应用不会因浏览动物而自动打开该网页。

若未来加入账户、广告、分析工具、联网功能或付费解锁，我们会在发布前重新核对实际数据流，更新本政策和 Google Play“数据安全”申报。政策更新时将修改此页面和修订日期。隐私疑问请联系 chen_sxue@163.com。

English

Data handled in the app

Dongdong Zoo does not require an account. Artwork, prerecorded spoken names, and call recordings for the 24 animals are included with the app. Taps, animal selection, muting, and the slideshow work on the device. A parent's interface-language choice is written to the app's local settings so it can be restored next time. The current version does not send names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, usage records, photos, or recordings from the app to the developer or third parties, and does not sell or share that data.

The app has no ads, analytics, tracking tools, or in-app purchases. Spoken names and calls are prerecorded; the app does not use the microphone or request camera or location permission. App content works offline.

Children and parents

The app is intended for children ages 2 and up to use with a parent or caregiver. It does not ask children for personal details and has no chat, upload, or social sharing feature. A parent can help with the controls and set a comfortable device volume.

Retention, deletion, and security

The app does not send or store user records on a developer server, so the developer has no app account or interaction records to retrieve. The language choice can be changed to follow the device language; its local copy can also be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. If Android system backup is enabled, the operating system may back up this preference to the cloud or transfer it to a new device under the user's backup settings. The user manages any system backup copy through device or account backup settings. The app itself does not upload personal data to a developer server.

If you choose to email us at chen_sxue@163.com, your email address and message are processed through email services. We use them only to reply and handle your request, not for advertising. We retain messages only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and delete them once it is resolved, unless applicable law requires otherwise. You may request access to or deletion of that correspondence by emailing us. Access to the mailbox is limited, and we use the security measures provided by the email service.

External services and changes

Google handles Play Store downloads, installs, and store services under its own policies. If you choose to open this page, GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical visit data under GitHub's privacy statement. Browsing animals in the app does not automatically open this page.

Before adding accounts, ads, analytics, online features, or a paid unlock, we will review the actual data flows and update this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration. Changes will appear on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions, email chen_sxue@163.com.
