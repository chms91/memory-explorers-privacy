PocketBank Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

PocketBank is a local pocket-money ledger for parents. It records balances and entries on your device.

Data stored on your device

PocketBank stores the child profile names you enter, currency, balances, ledger entries and notes, allowance rules, jar balances, and app preferences in private app storage. If you set a parent PIN, PocketBank stores a salted hash of it. Kid view is a read-only view of the same local ledger. PocketBank has no accounts and does not send ledger data to the developer.

Backups and CSV sharing

A backup contains the child profiles, balances, history, and allowance rules. You choose the file location. Backup files can be read by anyone who can access the file, so keep them private. Parent PINs and purchase status are kept on the device and are excluded from backups.

When you export a CSV, PocketBank passes it to Android's sharing menu. The file may contain ledger entries and notes. You choose where to save or send it. Any service you choose can apply its own data practices.

Retention and deletion

The ledger stays on your device until you clear PocketBank's app storage or uninstall the app. Backup files and CSV copies are separate files. Delete those copies from the apps or locations where you saved or sent them.

Advertising, analytics, and contact

PocketBank does not use advertising or third-party analytics, and the developer does not sell ledger data.

For support or privacy questions, contact mysticsky91@outlook.com. If you email support, your address and message are used to respond to your request. Please leave out child names, PINs, and private ledger files.
