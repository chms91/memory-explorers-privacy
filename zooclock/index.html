Zoo Clock Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. This policy applies to Zoo Clock: Tell Time (Android package com.mystic13.zooclock).

Information and device storage

Zoo Clock works offline. It saves game progress and preferences in private app storage on your device. This includes stars, sticker progress, the chosen theme, and audio and learning preferences. Zoo Clock does not send those records to the developer or share them with third parties. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Optional voice instructions

If English voice is enabled and a compatible offline voice is available, Zoo Clock passes its own English lesson text to the Android text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Zoo Clock selects a voice that does not require a network connection. It does not use your microphone, record your voice, or pass personal information to the speech engine. You can turn voice instructions off in Settings.

Children and families

Zoo Clock is designed for children ages 5 to 8. The app does not ask children to enter names, email addresses, or other personal information.

Retention and deletion

Game records stay on your device until you clear them. Reset progress in the Grown-ups section to remove stars and sticker progress. To remove preferences as well, clear Zoo Clock's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Zoo Clock does not keep a server copy.

Contact

Parents and guardians can contact Zoo Clock support at mysticsky91@outlook.com with privacy questions. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond to your request. Please do not include a child's personal information.
