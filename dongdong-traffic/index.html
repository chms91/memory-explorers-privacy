DongDong Traffic — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 4 November 2026
Package: com.mystic13.dongdongtraffic
Developer: mystic13
The English version is primary; Simplified Chinese is provided for convenience.

DongDong Traffic includes pictures, videos and spoken names for 20 vehicles. The first 8 are free; the other 12 can be unlocked with one non-consumable Google Play purchase. There is no subscription. No separate app account is required. The app has no ads, analytics, chat or upload feature and does not use the microphone, camera or location.

Language preferences and vehicle discoveries are stored locally. To verify and restore paid access, the app also stores a Google Play signed purchase receipt, including purchase and product identifiers and its signature, and verifies it on the device. These receipts are not uploaded to a developer server. We do not operate an app-account or purchase-verification backend.

Google Play handles purchases, restoration and online ownership checks, requiring internet and a Google Play account. Google processes payments and related information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not receive card numbers or payment passwords. The developer can access transaction and order-management information provided by Google Play Console for support and refunds. Verified paid content works offline. Refunds or revoked purchases are reflected after a subsequent successful Play ownership check.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local language preferences, discoveries and cached receipts. Restore purchases using the original Google Play account; this does not restore discovery progress. Uninstalling does not refund a purchase or delete orders held by Google. System backup behavior depends on the operating system and app backup configuration. The Android app disables standard automatic app backup (allowBackup=false). Restore purchases on another device using the original Google Play account.

The app is intended for children to enjoy with a parent or caregiver. A parental gate protects purchase and restore options. The app does not ask children for personal details. Parents can configure purchase authentication in Google Play.

If you email support, your address and message are handled by the email service and used only to respond and resolve your request, not for advertising. We delete correspondence after handling the request unless retention is legally required. Contact chen_sxue@163.com to request access to or deletion of this correspondence. Access to the support mailbox is restricted.

If you choose to open the public privacy webpage, GitHub Pages may process technical access data under its privacy policy. Browsing vehicles does not open this webpage automatically. Changes to data handling will be reflected in this document and the public policy.


Review access is separate from Google Play purchases. Users may enter a review access code in the parent area. The app stores this credential locally and revalidates it on startup for offline review and continued access after restart. The credential is not sent to a developer server and does not create a purchase order. Exiting review access deletes the local review credential and restores access based on actual purchases or the free content limits.

咚咚交通工具——隐私政策
生效日期：2026年11月4日

咚咚交通工具提供20种交通工具的图片、视频和名称语音。前8种免费，其余12种可通过Google Play一次性购买解锁，无订阅。应用无需自己的注册账号，没有广告、分析、聊天或上传功能，不使用麦克风、相机或定位。

语言选择和交通工具发现记录保存在设备本地。为了验证及恢复付费访问，应用还保存Google Play提供的已签名购买凭证，包括购买标识、商品标识和签名，并在本机重新验签。应用不会向开发者服务器上传这些凭证。开发者不运营本应用的用户账号或购买验证服务器。

购买、恢复和联网核对所有权由Google Play服务处理，需要网络及Google Play账号。Google按照其条款和隐私政策处理付款及相关信息。应用不会读取银行卡号码或付款密码。开发者可通过Google Play Console获得Google提供的交易及订单管理信息，用于支持和退款处理。已验证的付费内容可离线使用；退款或撤销会在之后成功查询Google Play所有权时同步。

清除应用数据或卸载会删除本地语言、发现记录和购买凭证；恢复购买使用原来的Google Play账号，不恢复交通工具发现进度。删除应用不会自动退款或删除Google保存的订单。设备系统备份行为受操作系统及应用备份设置控制；Android应用关闭标准自动备份（allowBackup=false）；换设备请使用原Google Play账号恢复购买。

本应用适合孩子与家长共同使用。购买和恢复入口设置家长验证；应用不要求儿童提供个人资料。家长可在Google Play设置购买身份验证。

若您主动发送支持邮件，邮箱地址和邮件内容由邮件服务处理，仅用于回复及处理请求，不用于广告；处理完成后删除，适用法律另有要求的除外。您可发送邮件请求查阅或删除相关往来。我们限制支持邮箱访问。
