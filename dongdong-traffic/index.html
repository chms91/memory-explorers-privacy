DongDong Traffic — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 19 October 2026
The English text is the primary version. The Simplified Chinese translation is provided for convenience.

1. Scope and contact
This policy explains the Android DongDong Traffic app (package com.mystic13.dongdongtraffic). It covers the app and voluntary support correspondence. For privacy questions, a parent or caregiver may contact chen_sxue@163.com.

2. Offline app; no account
The app contains 20 vehicles with pictures, spoken names in 10 languages, and videos. All media is included in the installation. The app does not need an account or an internet connection for these features. The voice generation service was used during production only; the installed app does not contact it or send it user data.

3. What the app does and does not collect
The app does not collect, transmit, share or sell personal information, device identifiers, location, usage analytics, photos or recordings. It has no advertisements, analytics or tracking SDK, purchases, chat, user-generated uploads or account registration. It does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or advertising ID access.

4. Data stored on the device
The app stores the chosen language and the set of discovered vehicles in its private local settings so that they remain available after reopening the app. These settings are not sent to us or to a third party. Media playback uses local installed files. The Android app disables the standard automatic app backup option (allowBackup=false); the app does not implement its own cloud backup or synchronization. Device-manufacturer transfer and operating-system features may have separate behavior and policies.

5. Retention and deletion
Local settings remain on the device until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. To remove them, use Android Settings > Apps > DongDong Traffic > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. This resets language and discovery progress. There is no online account or server-side app profile to delete.

6. Children and families
The app is designed for children to explore vehicles with a parent or caregiver. We do not ask children for personal information and do not knowingly collect it through the app. Parents should handle support correspondence and avoid sending a child's name, photo or other unnecessary personal details. If you believe child-related personal information has been sent to our support address, contact us to request its deletion.

7. Voluntary support email
If a parent or caregiver emails us, we receive the sender's address, the message, and any attachments voluntarily included. We use this information only to answer the request and resolve the issue. Email providers process and store messages under their own terms. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for advertising. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up; deletion can be requested at the address above, subject to any applicable legal retention obligation. Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

8. External services and websites
No external web page, analytics service or voice service is required for in-app playback. If you separately open a website or use your email provider, that service may process information such as network logs under its own privacy policy. This is separate from data collection by the installed app; it does not mean that the app contains that service's SDK. The public policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit that page, GitHub may process technical access logs under its privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This is separate from app analytics; the installed app does not automatically open the policy website.

9. Security and changes
Local settings use Android's app storage protections. We do not operate a server holding app activity or user profiles. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed absolutely secure. If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before the changed practices take effect. The effective date above identifies this version.

咚咚交通工具——隐私政策
生效日期：2026年10月19日
本政策以英文版为准，简体中文译文仅供参考。

1. 适用范围与联系
本政策说明Android版DongDong Traffic（咚咚交通工具，包名com.mystic13.dongdongtraffic）以及自愿发送的客服邮件如何处理信息。隐私问题请由家长或照护者联系chen_sxue@163.com。

2. 离线使用，无需账号
应用包含20种交通工具的图片、10种语言的名称语音及视频。素材全部随应用安装，使用这些功能无需账号或网络。语音生成服务只在素材制作阶段使用；安装后的应用不会调用该服务或向其发送用户数据。

3. 应用不收集的信息
应用不收集、传输、分享或出售个人信息、设备标识符、位置、使用分析、照片或录音。没有广告、分析或追踪SDK、应用内购买、聊天、用户内容上传或账号注册。不请求麦克风、相机、位置、通讯录或广告标识符权限。

4. 本机保存的信息
应用在本机私有设置中保存语言选择和已发现的交通工具，以便下次继续使用，不发送给我们或第三方。媒体播放读取已安装的本地文件。Android应用关闭标准自动应用备份选项（allowBackup=false），也没有自行实现云备份或同步。设备厂商迁移及操作系统功能可能有各自的行为和政策。

5. 保留与删除
本机设置保留至清除应用存储或卸载应用。可在Android“设置 > 应用 > DongDong Traffic > 存储 > 清除存储”中删除（不同设备名称可能不同），也可卸载应用。这会重置语言和发现记录。应用没有需要删除的在线账号或服务器用户档案。

6. 儿童与家庭
应用供儿童在家长或照护者陪伴下探索交通工具。我们不通过应用向儿童索取个人信息，也不通过应用有意收集儿童个人信息。请家长处理客服联系，不要发送儿童姓名、照片或其他不必要的个人信息。如认为曾向客服邮箱发送儿童个人信息，可联系我们申请删除。

7. 自愿发送的客服邮件
家长或照护者主动发送邮件时，我们会收到发件地址、邮件正文及主动附带的附件，仅用于回复和处理问题。邮件服务商会按照各自条款处理和保存邮件。我们不出售客服邮件，也不用其投放广告。邮件仅在处理请求及必要跟进所需期间保留；可通过上述邮箱申请删除，但适用法律要求保留的情况除外。请勿发送不必要的个人或敏感信息。

8. 外部服务与网站
应用内播放不依赖外部网页、分析服务或语音服务。您另行访问网站或使用邮箱时，相应服务可能按其政策处理网络日志等信息。这与已安装应用的数据收集不同，并不表示应用包含其SDK。本政策的网页副本可能产生由托管服务商处理的访问日志，具体取决于所选托管服务，不属于应用使用分析。

9. 安全与政策更新
本机设置由Android应用存储机制保护。我们没有保存应用活动或用户档案的服务器。任何存储或通信方式均无法保证绝对安全。如数据处理方式发生改变，我们会在改变生效前更新本政策及Google Play披露。以上生效日期标识当前版本。
